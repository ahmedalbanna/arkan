# 🎯 اقتراحات قوالب جديدة لـ Report Mode

## 📚 القوالب الأكاديمية المقترحة

### 1. Master's Thesis Template (رسالة ماجستير)
**الأهمية**: عالية جداً - مطلوب من طلاب الدراسات العليا

**البنية المقترحة**:
```
- Front Matter (Title, Abstract, Dedication, Acknowledgments)
- Chapter 1: Introduction
  • Background and Context
  • Problem Statement  
  • Research Questions/Hypotheses
  • Research Objectives
  • Significance of the Study
  • Scope and Limitations
  • Thesis Structure

- Chapter 2: Literature Review
  • Theoretical Framework
  • Review of Related Studies
  • Research Gap Analysis
  • Conceptual Framework

- Chapter 3: Research Methodology
  • Research Design
  • Population and Sampling
  • Data Collection Methods
  • Data Analysis Techniques
  • Validity and Reliability
  • Ethical Considerations

- Chapter 4: Results and Findings
  • Descriptive Statistics
  • Inferential Analysis
  • Research Questions/Hypotheses Testing
  • Additional Findings

- Chapter 5: Discussion
  • Interpretation of Results
  • Comparison with Previous Studies
  • Theoretical Implications
  • Practical Implications
  • Study Limitations

- Chapter 6: Conclusion and Recommendations
  • Summary of Findings
  • Conclusions
  • Recommendations for Practice
  • Recommendations for Future Research

- References
- Appendices
```

**Project Config**:
- projectType: 'Master\'s Thesis'
- educationalLevel: 'Graduate'

**Word Count**: 15,000-25,000 كلمة
**Complexity**: 85-95
**Formality**: 95-100

```
  • Research Approach
  • Research Strategy
  • Data Collection

  • Quality Criteria

- Chapter 5: Results/Findings (Study 2) [إذا كان متعدد الدراسات]

- Chapter 7: Conclusion
  • Summary

  • Future Research Directions

- References
- Appendices
```

- projectType: 'PhD Dissertation'
