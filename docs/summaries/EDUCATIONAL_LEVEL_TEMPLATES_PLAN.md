# 🎓 خطة القوالب حسب المستوى التعليمي

## 📋 نظرة عامة

سنضيف **قوالب متخصصة** لكل مستوى تعليمي مع مراعاة:
- مستوى التعقيد المناسب
- حجم المشروع/التقرير
- المهارات البحثية المتوقعة
- متطلبات كل مرحلة

---

## 🎯 القوالب المقترحة حسب المستوى

### 1️⃣ High School (المرحلة الثانوية)
**Complexity**: 40-60  
**Word Count**: 1,000-3,000

#### القوالب المقترحة:
1. **High School Research Paper** (5-8 أقسام)
   - Introduction
   - Background Research
   - Methodology (بسيط)
   - Findings
   - Conclusion
   - Bibliography

2. **High School Science Project Report** (6-8 أقسام)
   - Title Page
   - Abstract (قصير 100 كلمة)
   - Introduction
   - Materials and Methods
   - Results
   - Discussion
   - Conclusion
   - References

3. **High School Book Report** (4-5 أقسام)
   - Introduction
   - Summary
   - Analysis
   - Personal Reflection
   - Conclusion

---

### 2️⃣ Undergraduate - First Year (السنة الأولى)
**Complexity**: 50-65  
**Word Count**: 1,500-4,000

#### القوالب المقترحة:
1. **First Year Essay** (4-6 أقسام)
   - Introduction with thesis statement
   - Body paragraphs (2-3)
   - Counter-argument (optional)
   - Conclusion
   - References

2. **First Year Lab Report** (مبسط عن Lab Report الحالي)
   - Simplified structure
   - More guidance
   - Basic calculations only

3. **First Year Literature Review** (4-5 أقسام)
   - Introduction
   - Theme 1
   - Theme 2
   - Comparison/Synthesis
   - Conclusion

---

### 3️⃣ Undergraduate - Second/Third Year (السنة الثانية/الثالثة)
**Complexity**: 65-75  
**Word Count**: 3,000-6,000

#### القوالب المقترحة:
1. **Intermediate Research Paper** (6-8 أقسام)
   - More sophisticated than first year
   - Deeper analysis required
   - Multiple sources integration

2. **Course Project Report** (7-9 أقسام)
   - Introduction
   - Literature Review
   - Methodology
   - Implementation/Analysis
   - Results
   - Discussion
   - Conclusion
   - References

3. **Field Study Report** (6-8 أقسام)
   - Introduction
   - Study Site/Context
   - Methods
   - Observations
   - Analysis
   - Conclusion
   - References

---

### 4️⃣ Undergraduate - Final Year (السنة الأخيرة)
**ملاحظة**: موجود بالفعل!
- ✅ Graduation Project (17 قسم)
- ✅ Computer Engineering Project (18 قسم)

**يمكن إضافة**:
1. **Senior Thesis** (10-12 قسم)
   - More academic than graduation project
   - Research-focused

---

### 5️⃣ Master's Level
**ملاحظة**: موجود بالفعل!
- ✅ Master's Thesis (35 قسم)

**يمكن إضافة**:
1. **Master's Comprehensive Exam Essay**
2. **Master's Literature Review** (أكثر تفصيلاً)

---

### 6️⃣ Doctoral Level
**Complexity**: 90-100  
**Word Count**: 50,000-100,000

#### القوالب المقترحة:
1. **PhD Dissertation** (12-15 فصل، 60-80 قسم)
   - Most comprehensive template
   - Multiple study chapters
   - Extensive literature review
   - Methodological contributions
   - Theoretical framework development

2. **PhD Proposal** (8-10 أقسام)
   - Introduction
   - Literature Review
   - Theoretical Framework
   - Research Questions/Hypotheses
   - Proposed Methodology
   - Expected Contributions
   - Timeline
   - References

3. **Doctoral Qualifying Exam Paper** (6-8 أقسام)

---

### 7️⃣ Post-Doctoral Level
**Complexity**: 95-100  
**Word Count**: varies

#### القوالب المقترحة:
1. **Post-Doctoral Research Proposal** (9-11 أقسام)
   - Background and Significance
   - Preliminary Studies
   - Research Design and Methods
   - Expected Outcomes
   - Timeline and Milestones
   - Budget Justification
   - References

2. **Grant Proposal** (10-12 قسم)
   - Project Summary
   - Project Description
   - Significance and Innovation
   - Approach
   - Preliminary Studies
   - Research Team
   - Budget and Justification

---

### 8️⃣ Professional Level
**ملاحظة**: العديد موجود!
- ✅ Business Proposal
- ✅ Case Study
- ✅ Feasibility Study
- ✅ White Paper (مقترح)

**يمكن إضافة**:
1. **Professional Certification Report**
2. **Industry Analysis Report**
3. **Policy Brief**
4. **Consulting Report**

## 💡 اعتبارات خاصة

### High School:
- Tone: educational, encouraging
- Formality: 60-75
- Complexity: 40-60
- More guidance and examples
- Simpler vocabulary

### First Year:
- Tone: instructional, supportive
- Formality: 70-80
- Complexity: 50-65
- Step-by-step guidance
- Basic academic writing

### Second/Third Year:
- Tone: academic, professional
- Formality: 75-85
- Complexity: 65-75
- More independence expected
- Deeper analysis

### Doctoral:
- Tone: scholarly, rigorous
- Formality: 95-100
- Complexity: 90-100
- Original contribution expected
- Theoretical sophistication

### Post-Doctoral:
- Tone: expert, innovative
- Formality: 95-100
- Complexity: 95-100
- Focus on funding/impact
- Collaboration emphasis
